# Habit Quest: Art Spec

How to draw art for the hero and gear, and how to swap it into the app. **You never need to touch code to replace art.**

## 1. The canvas
- Every frame is **256 × 320 px**, with a transparent background.
- **Anchor:** the hero's feet rest on **x = 128, y = 300**. Every layer is drawn against this same position, so layers line up when stacked.
- The hero roughly fills **x 32–224, y 30–300**. Leave room around the edges for swings and glows.
- Export **PNG** at 1× (256 × 320). If you want crisper art on retina phones, draw at 2× and downscale to these exact sizes.

## 2. Animations are sprite strips
- An animation is one PNG with its frames **side by side, left to right**. A 4-frame animation is a 1024 × 320 PNG.
- `idle` is required for every art id. It can be a single frame, and the app adds a gentle breathing bob on top.
- `fight` is optional. Weapons use it to swing (placeholders have 4 frames at 8 fps). A layer without `fight` uses its `idle` art while fighting.

## 3. Layers, back to front
| # | Layer | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `aura` | glow behind the hero when rare+ gear is equipped (`fx.aura.rare`, `fx.aura.epic`, `fx.aura.legendary`) |
| 2 | `accessoryBack` | accessory slot (cloaks, charms) |
| 3 | `legs` | `base.legs` (trousers and plain shoes) |
| 4 | `feet` | feet slot (boots), drawn over the plain shoes |
| 5 | `bodyBase` | `base.body` (linen shirt, arms, hands) |
| 6 | `body` | body slot (tunic, armour), covering the shirt and sleeves; leave the hands visible |
| 7 | `head` | `base.head` (head and ears) |
| 8 | `face` | `base.face` (eyes, blush, mouth) |
| 9 | `hair` | `base.hair` |
| 10 | `headGear` | head slot (hats, helms) |
| 11 | `weapon` | weapon slot, held in the right hand at about (164, 255) on the canvas |

## 4. Files and names
- Every image lives at `assets/art/<art id>/<animation>.png`. Examples:
  - `assets/art/base.hair/idle.png`
  - `assets/art/gear.iron-sword/idle.png`
  - `assets/art/gear.iron-sword/fight.png`
- Gear art ids are `gear.<item id>`. Item ids are listed in `src/content/gear.ts`.

## 5. Replacing a placeholder with your art
1. Draw your layer on the 256 × 320 canvas, with the feet on the anchor.
2. Export it as PNG and **save over** the placeholder file with the same path and name.
3. If your animation has a different number of frames, open `src/art/manifest.ts`, find the art id, and change `frames` (and `fps` if you like).
4. Reload the app (press `r` in the Expo terminal).

## 6. Checking your art
- In a development build, open the **Character** tab and tap **Art preview**.
- It shows the hero with any combination of gear, in both `idle` and `fight`, so you can check alignment.

## 7. Placeholders
- The placeholders are generated by `scripts/gen-placeholders.mjs` in exactly this format.
- Running it again only creates **missing** files, so it never overwrites your art.
- Two options change that: `--force` regenerates every placeholder and **will overwrite your art**, and `--manifest` rewrites `src/art/manifest.ts`.
